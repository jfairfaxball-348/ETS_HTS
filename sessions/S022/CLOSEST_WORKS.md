## Exact three-language translation checks and logical direction
1. **Ellipse ↔ anisotropic circle.** (P(\theta)=(a\cos\theta,b\sin\theta)), so squared distance between transformed circular sites is (a^2(\Delta\cos)^2+b^2(\Delta\sin)^2). Mapping sites and preserved segment crossings works both ways at positive a,b **if** this quadratic metric is also transported. Mapping to the *ordinary isotropic circle distance* loses rankings, so no naive circular theorem subsumes ellipse safety.
2. **Nearest-two chronological graph ↔ recursive metric-ear certificates** on strictly convex sites only: last degree two, 2n−3 outerplanar saturation forces hull-neighbour attachments ⇒ metric ear and recursive earlier drawing. Conversely reverse a COMPLETE deletion certificate ⇒ plane chronological rule. Ordinary ear clipping ⇒ metric-ear is **false**, witness S016 exact five-point ellipse with all vertices exposed and no metric ear. Hence ordinary triangulation theorems cannot be silently promoted to our iff.
3. **Quantifiers near a circle:** CLM-0103 `for each n ∃ epsilon_n >0, ∀ n-sites safe` does **not** entail CLM-0107 `∃ epsilon>0, ∀n, ∀ n-sites safe`. Formal countermodel to the *quantifier implication*: take abstract thresholds epsilon_n=1/n; each n positive but inf_n epsilon_n=0. This is an illustration of invalid inference, not a geometric counterexample.
4. **Universal safe nonround ↔ false circle-only conjecture:** B at any r>1 ⇒ existence of one noncircle universally safe ⇒ negate “only circle”. Reverse is only a logical existence at *some* r>1, and does NOT recover our explicit interval or 101/100, unless a strengthening is supplied.
