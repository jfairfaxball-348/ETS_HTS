## Proof structure in S020/S021
For k≥5 sites, parameter gaps `g_j>0` sum to `2π`. Two-gap spans `s_j=g_{j-1}+g_j` sum to `4π`; some `s_j≤4π/5<Λ` even at r=R. Among eligible short `s_j<Λ`, minimise the **actual Euclidean skip-one chord** `L_j=|P_{j-1}-P_{j+1}|`, not angular span. Adjacent chords strictly shorter. A competing non-neighbour falls into forward-short, central-closed, or backward-short angular band and has distance `≥L_i`; central band uses `|chord|≥2b sin(Λ/2)≥2a sin(2π/5)`. Thus strict metric ear in every surviving k-set; recursive deletion to 4-point base builds valid chronological order. `R≈1.0502021` is **sufficient** only: optimality, converse for r>R, and complete ratio classification unproved.

## Three nontrivial translations and logical direction checks

1. **Incremental nearest-neighbour / ordered graph.** Given the CLM0107 order, keeping only one of the two compulsory new edges yields some ordered **1-NNG** subgraph. Reverse fails: a one-nearest construction is a tree (after initial connectivity conventions) and imposes no noncrossing condition on the compulsory *second* predecessor edge. None of the inspected 1-NNG degree papers implies this main result.
2. **Maximal outerplanar metric-ear certificate.** An n-site plane retained 2-nearest graph has exactly `2n−3` genuine edges. In strict convex position, standard outerplanar edge cap implies hull edges present; final chronological vertex's two edges must be its cyclic hull sides and must be exactly its ranked nearest predecessors. Remove it; repeat. Conversely a **full** sequence of metric ears yields an allowed insertion order by reversal. A standard geometric ear triangulation is not enough: it imposes no Euclidean rank; thus the classical two-ears theorem alone does not subsume CLM0099 or CLM0107.
3. **Greedy/Delaunay/Yao/θ.** Greedy triangulation can reject an intersecting edge; θ/Yao selects closest predecessor within a cone (usually one per cone); Delaunay uses empty circumcircles over complete sites; static k-NNG uses **all** sites' neighbours. None enforces the same two global nearest predecessors on each arrival with unconditionally retained edges. Graph families intersect but are not identical; no proof of equivalent quantifiers or implications located.
4. **Circle with anisotropic metric.** Map a unit-circle site `u=(cos θ,sin θ)` to ellipse site `(a cos θ,b sin θ)`. Incidence, cyclic order and crossings are affine-invariant, but the relevant squared length becomes `a²(dx)²+b²(dy)²`. A circular *ordinary Euclidean* nearest-ear theorem cannot be transported by the affine map: ranks can change. A **known theorem about ALL circle subsets under these anisotropic metrics with r≤R** WOULD subsume CLM0107, and no inspected one was located.

No translation itself certifies novelty; the unknown literature may use different terminology.
