## Structural comparison: CLM0099
The `2n−3` cap and maximal outerplanar-graph triangulation facts are standard mathematical background, and basic geometric ear deletion is familiar. They do **not** independently establish either direction of the **exact nearest-two metric-ear iff** without the extra rank argument. The internal S021 derivation proves necessity by counting hull-side saturation + actual last chronological vertex, and sufficiency by recursively reversing an entire certificate. No independently inspected literature statement specifically including this metric ranking and **iff** was located. **Do not** assert it is original: an elementary specialisation in an uninspected article is plausible.

## Numerical comparison: R
The sufficient threshold `sqrt(2/sin(2π/5)−1)` comes from a chord envelope and the worst `k=5` average two-gap span; `101/100` is a proper subinterval witness. We found **no inspected older source claiming exactly this ellipse-ratio bound**, but the value is not asserted optimal and its formula may arise from elementary geometry in a different variable (e.g. eccentricity `e²=1−(b/a)²`). Neither search absence nor Lean proves novelty of the number.
