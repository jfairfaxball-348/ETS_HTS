## Owner-reviewable prospective paper / Palomar project title
Noncrossing Two-Nearest-Predecessor Orders on Nearly Circular Ellipses

## Suggested exact public plain-text description
We prove that every finite set of at least three distinct points on an ellipse with semiaxes a >= b > 0 and a/b <= sqrt(2/sin(2*pi/5) - 1) admits a noncrossing insertion order under a compulsory Euclidean two-nearest-predecessor rule. The first three sites form a triangle. Each subsequent site is joined to exactly its two nearest earlier sites, with Cartesian-lexicographic resolution of distance ties, and all existing edges are retained. The sufficient aspect-ratio condition includes its endpoint, applies to arbitrarily many and arbitrarily close distinct sites, and includes the explicit noncircular case a/b = 101/100. The source formalization also establishes a full recursive metric-ear certificate characterisation for finite strictly convex-position sets, complete three/four-point cases, and short directed ellipse-chord estimates. Neither sharpness nor historical priority is claimed.

## Headline paper theorem
R = sqrt(2/sin(2*pi/5) - 1) ~ 1.0502020988726573.
For any 0<b<=a and a/b<=R, any finite S subset E(a,b), |S|>=3, admits a noncrossing retained chronological two-Euclidean-nearest-predecessor insertion order, including the tie rule, initial triangle, all cardinalities and closed R.
