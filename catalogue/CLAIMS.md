## S006 additional claims
- **CLM-0038** [PROGRAMME_PROVED]: Any length-2q doubled-colour word with unequal neighbours has a fair two-person splitting with at most q−1 cuts. Justification: Choose independent fair orientation per colour: first occurrence goes to owner X_a, second to the other. Every adjacent pair uses distinct colours so its ownership transition is a fair Bernoulli variable. E(cuts)=(2q−1)/2=q−1/2, implying an integer outcome <=q−1. Cut at changes. Limits: Full universal human proof in S006/MATHEMATICS.md, not independent review; computational cases supplementary, no originality claim. References: SRC-0036; CND-0068; CAM-0001, CAM-0002.
- **CLM-0039** [PROGRAMME_PROVED]: The CND-0080 claimed maximum incidence fails on an explicit simple six-gon. Justification: Vertices cyclically (0,10),(-2,0),(-1,3),(0,4),(1,3),(2,0). The lower chain has slopes 3,1,-1,-3. All nonconsecutive lower-chain chords lie outside; the sole triangulation is apex fan and loads top vertex with 4 triangles, above ceil(6/2)=3. Limits: Independent coordinate argument; no computational certification or referee. A counterexample, not a new open theorem. References: SRC-0037; CND-0080; no CAM.
- **CLM-0040** [PROGRAMME_PROVED]: The cycle C5 lacks an additive-one tree spanner. Justification: Every spanning tree of C5 is a path created by deleting one cycle edge. Its two original endpoints are distance 4 in the tree versus 1 in the graph. Limits: Simple human counterexample; no novel general result. References: SRC-0043; CND-0082; no CAM.

References: SRC-0038; CND-0083; no CAM.
