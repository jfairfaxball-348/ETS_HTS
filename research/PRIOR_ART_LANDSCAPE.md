# Prior-art landscape — search map, not a completed search

**Scope warning:** S001 catalogues *where to look*, not what a live search returned. No source has yet been inspected or assigned SRC; all claimed result counts remain unspecified. Field classification below is methodological judgement rather than a novelty conclusion.

| Landscape | What it covers / why useful | Saturation risk | Blind spots / search approach |
|---|---|---|---|
| OEIS | Numerical finite-case fingerprints, terms, keywords and offsets | High for short integer sequences; false matches from prefixes | Search text and transform sequences; inspect commentary and references |
| arXiv full text | Contemporary specialist preprints and phrasing | High for standard extremal mutations | Exact operators, translated terminology, 2025–26 date filter |
| MathOverflow / Math.SE | Folklore, answered variants, expert reductions | High for attractive classroom questions | Search synonyms, comments and accepted answers |
| Erdős Problems | Crisp combinatorial/number-theory frontiers | Very high near famous named problems | Search surrounding stronger/weaker bounds and links |
| Open Problem Garden / Wikipedia lists | Public problem descriptions and hyperlinks | High for familiar one-paragraph conjectures | Treat summaries as leads, not statement inspection |
| Guy, *Unsolved Problems in Number Theory* | Arithmetic, digit processes, Diophantine families | Very high for elegant integer mutations | Check editions, references and changed solved status |
| Croft–Falconer–Guy, *Unsolved Problems in Geometry* | Geometric colouring, packing, arrangement | Very high for natural shapes and configurations | Translate into graph/incidence language |
| Brass–Moser–Pach | Discrete geometric problems | Very high for planar combinatorics | Look at parameter variants and bibliographies |
| Kourovka notebook | Group-theoretic questions and folklore | High in group theory, less applicable elsewhere | Search precise group constraints when generated |
| Mathematics Magazine / AMM problems | Accessible, concise problems with short solutions | Very high for elegant olympiad-style tasks | Check solution issues and rephrasings |
| 2025–2026 preprints | Fast-moving prior statements | Unknown until actually inspected | Search dated papers, revisions and adjacent titles |
| Zenodo / GitHub research repos | Experimental statements and unpublished observations | Hard to index, low formal visibility | Search README/text/code keywords, distinguish claim status |

**Over-mined zones:** base changes to Collatz, small numeric tweaks to famous conjectures, planar colouring thresholds, familiar unit-fraction identities, classical finite board tilings and entries from named Erdős lists. These frequently hide either a known general theorem or many previously checked variants.

**Potentially less directly indexed zones (not necessarily less researched):** genuinely coupled constraints on one structure, interactions between elementary processes, exact minimal witnesses with two independent restrictions, and parameter boundaries between dynamics and finite graph invariants. Less searchable is *not* equivalent to unstudied. Exploit alternative languages, citation chaining, thesis/catalogue searches and negative-result records.

All future queries belong to per-candidate search logs, not retrospective recollection; exact result counts cannot be invented. Literature access levels and copyright limitations must be recorded.
