## Claim-by-claim audit (all five)

- **CLM0107 — principal ellipse theorem:** all finite `n ≥ 3`, arbitrary distinct sites on an ellipse with `a ≥ b > 0` and `a/b ≤ R=sqrt(2/sin(2π/5)−1)` **including equality**, existence of an insertion ordering with **exactly the two Euclidean nearest earlier sites** selected at every later arrival, original Cartesian lexicographic distance ties, all prior edges retained and no proper crossings. Distinguish existence of an order from arbitrary/random insertion, ordinary static nearest-neighbour graphs and changed crossing/retention definitions. **Do not assert R is optimal.**
- **CLM0108 — exact nonround all-n example:** `a/b=101/100`; evaluate independent novelty and whether it is a direct corollary of a known, stronger general theorem.
- **CLM0099 — strictly convex finite-set characterisation:** *if and only if* existence of a plane retained nearest-two insertion order and a **complete recursively valid metric-ear deletion certificate**. Look for genuinely equivalent inductive elimination, shelling, ear decompositions or outerplanar ordering characterisations, not just terminology.
- **CLM0096 — true arbitrary three-/four-site results:** verify any uniqueness, ordering, general-position and crossing clauses against classic finite planar triangulation and greedy-ordering facts. Report if elementary/known, regardless of formal proof effort.
- **CLM0102 — geometric lemma:** both outward endpoint derivatives and **strict** short-directed-arc chord extension, with exact ellipse/real-lift hypotheses. Identify prior differential-geometric, convex-curve, arc monotonicity, circle/ellipse chord and curvature arguments; determine independent originality versus standard technique.

Also separately assess the **actual proof technique and threshold `R`**: whether these (including closed-endpoint quantification) have direct antecedents, and whether the overall theorem merely combines standard lemmas. Assess each claim's *mathematical*, not only bibliographic, relationship to the others.
